# v1.5.0 Learning Science Research

- Researched at: 2026-08-14
- Goal: turn source-grounded webpages from passive summaries into short, repeatable learning loops.
- Catalog discovery: the unified runner failed on Windows because it invoked `npx` rather than `npx.cmd`; direct catalog calls then timed out through the configured proxy. Catalog popularity and rating comparisons are `missing evidence`.

## Primary research

| Source | Evidence used | Adopt | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Dunlosky et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266) | Practice testing and distributed practice received high-utility assessments; self-explanation and interleaving received moderate-utility assessments. | Retrieval questions, spaced review, selective teach-back. | Do not upgrade moderate evidence into universal claims. |
| [Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x) | In delayed tests, prior testing produced better retention than repeated studying. | Questions before answers; do not make rereading the default loop. | One study does not define a universal review interval. |

## Relevant skills inspected

- **starline-pdf-essence**: keep its 2-minute overview, ordered core line, recall and action sections; adapt them into a cross-format, reusable loop with hidden answers and review state.
- **ship-learn-next** (already inspected in the package prior-art report): keep active recall and one next action; continue rejecting a mandatory multi-week quest for every note.
- **multimedia-learning-design** (already inspected): keep signaling, contiguity and segmentation; do not add media quotas or decorative diagrams.

## Contribution ledger

- **keep**: retrieval practice, distributed review, 2-minute orientation and a real next action.
- **adapt**: Feynman-style teaching becomes a concrete self-explanation prompt with a named audience; 80/20 becomes navigation only.
- **reject**: fixed forgetting percentages, passive rereading as the main path, gamified streaks, and visible answers before recall.
- **invent**: a cross-page six-step learning loop, local-only teach-back/review state, wording that distinguishes “reviewed” from “mastered”, and an automated contract test.

## Evidence status

- **Validated advantage**: package tests can verify required contract language and output cases.
- **Design advantage**: the workflow now requires retrieval, teach-back, transfer and adjustable review before delivery.
- **Hypothesis**: the pages may improve long-term retention; a controlled learner outcome study remains `missing evidence`.
